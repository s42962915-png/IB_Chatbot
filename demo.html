InBody Knowledge Assistant

Try the floating chat bubble in the bottom-right corner.

Embed on any website

One script tag adds the chat widget to any page:

<script src="/widget.js" defer></script>

For an external site, use the full URL of your deployed server:

<script src="https://your-host.example.com/widget.js" defer></script>

What it answers

The assistant answers strictly from the approved InBody documents in the configured knowledge base, and shows source filenames where available. It declines to guess when the documents do not support an answer.
